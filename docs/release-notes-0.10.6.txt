Colab Extras 0.10.6

Left-clicking blank space outside selected file/folder rows clears the selection. Right-clicking blank space keeps it; right-clicking a selected row keeps the group, and another row uses its own context. Selection menus and the deletion confirmation preserve the group when clicked.

Group context menus now show only supported batch actions: currently Delete selected items. Single-item native and extension commands and separators are hidden and excluded from keyboard navigation. Single-item menus retain their normal actions. Command/Ctrl selection, subtle highlights, and confirmed bulk deletion remain available without extra sidebar controls.
